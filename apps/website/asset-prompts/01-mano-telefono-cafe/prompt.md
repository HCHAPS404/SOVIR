# 01 — Mano con teléfono en café

**Archivo destino:** `apps/website/public/photos/mano-telefono-cafe.webp` · **Aspecto:** 3:4 (vertical)

## Prompt (EN)

```
Editorial lifestyle photograph, over-the-shoulder close shot of a person's
hand holding a modern smartphone on a wooden café table, a ceramic coffee
cup softly out of focus in the background, warm morning window light from
the left, shallow depth of field (50mm, f/2.0), warm neutral color grade —
cream, ink black and soft amber tones, matte editorial look, no neon.

The phone screen is in sharp focus and shows a dark-themed security app:
near-black background, cream-colored text, a chat view with an incoming
message bubble, and at the bottom a warning card with a small amber dot
and the heading "Actúa con cuidado" followed by two short lines of Spanish
explanation text. Thin rounded cards, hairline borders, one amber accent.
The phone has a thin dark bezel and a centered pill-shaped camera cutout.

Realistic skin texture, natural relaxed grip, casual long-sleeve clothing,
blurred café interior in background. Photorealistic, shot on a mirrorless
camera, subtle film grain, no flash.
```

## Negative prompt

```
extra fingers, deformed hands, text in English, gibberish UI, bright neon
colors, holograms, matrix-style green code, glowing blue tech elements,
stock-photo smiles, people looking at camera, Apple/Samsung/Google logos,
iPhone-style camera notch, screen glare hiding the UI, watermark, cartoon,
3D render look
```
