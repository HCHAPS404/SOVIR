# 03 — Teléfono en la calle

**Archivo destino:** `apps/website/public/photos/telefono-calle.webp` · **Aspecto:** 3:4 (vertical)

## Prompt (EN)

```
Editorial street photograph, a person walking on a city sidewalk shot
from a low three-quarter angle, mid-stride, casually holding a smartphone
in one hand at waist level, the phone screen angled toward camera and in
focus showing a dark security app: near-black background, a warning card
with a small amber dot, cream-colored heading "Actúa con cuidado" and two
lines of small Spanish text, thin rounded borders, minimal.

Golden-hour city light, warm amber rim light on the hand and phone,
blurred storefronts and people in the background with soft bokeh, warm
neutral grade — cream, ink, soft amber — matte editorial finish,
photorealistic, 50mm f/1.8, natural candid posture, jacket or coat,
motion in the legs but a steady hand holding the phone.
```

## Negative prompt

```
looking at camera, posed, extra fingers, warped phone, English UI,
gibberish text, neon signs in focus, cyberpunk look, holograms, screen
glare, heavy traffic, sports/action framing, watermark, cartoon, brand
logos on the phone or clothing
```

## Notas (ES)

- El gesto clave: la mano tranquila sujetando el teléfono — protección
  "sin ruido", no una escena de peligro.
- Si el texto se deforma mucho, recorta y pega la tarjeta real encima.
