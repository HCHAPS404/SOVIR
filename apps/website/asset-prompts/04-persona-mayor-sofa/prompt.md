# 04 — Persona mayor en el sofá (bonus)

**Archivo destino:** `apps/website/public/photos/persona-mayor-sofa.webp` · **Aspecto:** 3:4 (vertical)

## Prompt (EN)

```
Editorial home photograph, an older adult (60s) sitting comfortably on a
fabric sofa in a warm living room, reading glasses on, holding a
smartphone with both hands at chest height, calm attentive expression,
the phone screen visible over their shoulder in soft focus showing a
dark security app with an amber-accented warning card and cream text on
near-black background.

Late afternoon window light, a knitted blanket over the sofa arm, a
side table with reading glasses case and a cup of tea, warm neutral
grade — cream, ink, soft amber — matte editorial look, photorealistic,
85mm f/2.0 portrait lens, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain,
dignified and calm mood — protection without fear.
```

## Negative prompt

```
fear or distress expression, dramatic shadows, English UI, gibberish
screen text, cluttered room, hospital/care-home look, posed studio
lighting, neon, extra fingers, watermark, cartoon, brand logos
```

## Notas (ES)

- Es la imagen emocional: SOVIR protege también a quien más lo necesita.
  La expresión debe ser **calma**, no miedo.
- Funciona muy bien en el pitch ("tu mamá también recibe estos mensajes").
